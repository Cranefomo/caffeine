How this works

What's the 50 mg line?

A rough rule of thumb, not a medical limit. Around 50 mg is about half an espresso. Below that most people sleep fine. Studies show high doses can disturb sleep even many hours before bed, and sensitive people notice much less.

Why does "half-life" matter so much?

Your body clears about half the caffeine every few hours. For the average healthy adult that's about 5 hours, but it ranges from about 1.5 to 9.5 hours. Smoking makes it faster. The pill and pregnancy make it much slower. Pick Fast or Slow above if that's you.

How accurate is it?

It's a simple model: each drink is absorbed in about 45 minutes, then cleared at your half-life. Real caffeine content varies a lot by brand and barista, so treat the numbers as a guide. Not medical advice.
